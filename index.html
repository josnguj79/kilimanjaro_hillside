Welcome to Paradise

An Eco-Friendly Refuge on the Slopes of Greatness

Perched on the lush foothills of Mount Kilimanjaro, our resort offers a refined blend of sustainable architecture, private botanical gardens, and panoramic views of Africa’s highest peak.

Whether returning from a summit trek or seeking a peaceful retreat away from city life, unwind in private villas crafted from local timber and stone, served by local culinary artists.
